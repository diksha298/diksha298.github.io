About Me

My name is Diksha Soni and I love to design, craft, and innovate everything related to henna! I bring traditional patterns, bridal mehendi, and Arabic art styles together in every design I create.

Practicing henna since childhood, what started as a hobby became a true passion. I have delighted over a thousand clients with personalised, intricate designs for weddings, festivals, and celebrations across Bengaluru.
